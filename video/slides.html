MandatePay

Give your AI agent a mandate, not a wallet.
Spending limits for AI agents, paid in tUSD8 · live on Aevum testnet (chain 44005)

AI agents are starting to pay for things

Option A: give it a funded wallet
One prompt injection, one bug, one leaked key, and the whole balance is gone.
Option B: approve every payment
Safe, but you are now the agent's payment clerk. The point of an agent is lost.
What a company gives an employee is neither: it is a corporate card with rules.

A mandate is a session key that can only…

  • pay allow-listed merchants
  • up to a per-payment cap and a rolling budget (aligned windows: the reset cannot be gamed)
  • until it expires, or the owner revokes it, or hits the kill switch for every agent
  • above a review threshold, payments wait for the owner's one-tap approval (funds reserved)
  • each invoice ref is single-use: a retrying agent cannot pay twice
  • the agent holds no gas: it signs an EIP-712 PaymentIntent, any relayer submits it
